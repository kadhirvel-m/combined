Circuit Logic: Series vs Parallel

Toggle the switches to observe how electricity flows.

Series Circuit

Current is Constant

Logic: In a series circuit, there is only ONE path. If the switch opens, or a bulb breaks, the entire circuit stops.

Status: Circuit Open (No Flow)

Parallel Circuit

Voltage is Constant

Logic: In parallel, current SPLITS. The top bulb works even if the bottom branch switch is off. If the Main switch is off, everything stops.

Status: Main Open
