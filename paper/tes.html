Binary Search

Finding a word in a dictionary by opening it in the middle

Instead of checking every page one by one, you flip to the middle, decide which half your word must be in, and ignore the other half. Repeat until you land exactly on it. That strategy is Binary Search.

Under the Hood

        


            

                
Definition

                
Binary Search finds a target in a sorted list by repeatedly dividing the search range in half.

            


            

                
Time Complexity

                
O(log n) — each step cuts the remaining elements in half.

            


            

                
Formula

                
mid = Math.floor((low + high) / 2)

            


            

                
Requirements

                
The array must be sorted. Otherwise results are invalid.

            


            

                
Common Mistake

                
Forgetting to update low/high correctly, causing infinite loops.

            


            

                
Real Uses

                
Databases, search engines, game engines, and nearly every fast lookup system.
