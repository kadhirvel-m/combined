Quick troubleshooting

Try these steps for the most common hiccups before writing to support.

  1. looks_one
    Refresh and re-upload the PDF. Ensure each unit heading is formatted consistently.
  2. looks_two
    For KaTeX errors, wrap math expressions in `$` or `$$` and avoid Word smart quotes.
  3. looks_3
    Check the Export pane for pending background tasks before downloading Markdown/PDF.

Top FAQs

Short answers to questions we get every day.

How do I invite my teammates to a project? add
Open the project → Team → Invite and share the link. Members join with campus email verification.
Can I undo a skill test submission? add
Tests auto-submit at time-out. You can request a reset within 30 minutes from the results page. Mentors can reopen attempts.
Where can I see my billing history? add
Go to Account → Billing. Download invoices, manage UPI mandates, and switch plans anytime.
Does Paper X work offline? add
Yes. Sync your syllabus once, then use notes and flashcards offline. Progress syncs when you reconnect.
