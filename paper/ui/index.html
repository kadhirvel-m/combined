Frequently asked questions

How do you reduce hallucinations? ▾

We use trusted‑source retrieval, reranking, and cross‑checks; every note block carries citations. Faculty can verify content via the console.

Can I study offline? ▾

Yes—download offline packs with notes, diagrams, and flashcards. Progress syncs when you're back online.

Does it support my language? ▾

We support English and are rolling out multilingual packs (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali) based on demand.
